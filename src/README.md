# AAF CareConnect™

**The UK's Connected Care Platform for fostering, residential care, training and compliance.**

AAF CareConnect™ is a modular, enterprise-grade cloud ecosystem for the UK fostering sector, focusing initially on Training, CPD, and Course Creation.

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- **Dashboard** — Administrator overview of learners, completions, compliance and CPD.
- **Learning Hub** — Course catalogue, learner progress and an immersive interactive course player (Safeguarding Children Level 2) with AI trainer, knowledge checks, scenario learning, final assessment and certificate download.
- **CPD & Certificates** — Track professional development records, monitor expiry, verify certificates and drive compliance.
- **Course Builder** — Block-based authoring of new training content.
- **Administration** — Users, organisation settings, module toggles and notifications.

## Vision

A single connected platform that unites fostering, residential care, training and compliance — starting with Learning and CPD in Phase 1, expanding to Form F Hub, Compliance Hub, Recruitment CRM, Workforce ERP and AI Assistant in later phases.
